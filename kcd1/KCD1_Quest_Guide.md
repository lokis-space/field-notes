# Kingdom Come: Deliverance — Every Quest, in the Order to Start It

Completionist route grouped by location, based on [u/MistaaIcyJ's list](https://www.reddit.com/r/kingdomcome/comments/zk82j3/list_of_all_quests_in_order_of_when_you_should/). 135 quests in 9 parts. Links go to kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg.

**Flags:** ⏳ timed · ⛔ point of no return · ⚖ mutually exclusive · ↪ moved from the post's position · ➕ added

## Part 1 — Prologue & Rattay

*Skalitz falls, Theresa's side of the story, and quick jobs around Rattay before you report to Sir Radzig.*

- [ ] **1. [Unexpected Visit](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Unexpected_Visit)** — `MAIN` — Henry's last ordinary morning in Skalitz.
  - *Where:* Starts automatically — Henry wakes at home in Skalitz; his father Martin is at the forge.
  1. Eat breakfast, then see Martin at the forge — he needs supplies to finish Sir Radzig's sword.
  2. Help friends: join Fritz, Matthew and the lads in their prank on the German's house.
  3. Get help from friends: bring them along when you squeeze Kunesh for the money he owes.
  4. Buy the charcoal and beer, then help Martin finish the sword.
  - **Recommended:** Do both friend objectives — they set up the Skalitz friendships that matter later.
  - **Outcome:** Tutorial with no lasting branches. Joining the dung prank earns a scolding from Martin but nothing worse; skipping it costs only some early friendship flavour.
  - **Tip:** Mother's opening questions set starting stats. Answer 'They are not drunks', then 'Give me an example' to unlock Martin's Kuttenberg talk. Buy the ale last – it warms.
  - **Immersion:** Let Henry be a lazy, cocky village lad here: sleep-heavy, eager for fights and pranks. Don't strip the house or steal the charcoal; that's not yet who he is.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Sigismund's army, Cumans included, falls on Skalitz without warning.</details>
- [ ] **2. [Run!](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Run!)** — `MAIN` — Flee the burning town and warn Talmberg.
  - *Where:* Starts automatically when the army reaches Skalitz.
  1. Grab what you can at home and take Radzig's sword.
  2. Escape the town: the gates are shut, so make for the stables.
  3. Ride for Talmberg with Cumans on your heels and warn the castle.
  - **Outcome:** Whistling to scare the Cumans off Theresa changes her later dialogue for the better. Asking Lady Stephanie for help is the cleanest escape; pleading with Robard costs reputation.
  - **Tip:** Ride without stopping – the arrow wound kills you if you dawdle. Leaving Talmberg: Stephanie points you to a guard disguise, then talk Radim into opening the gate.
  - **Immersion:** Never draw on the Cumans. At Talmberg, take only the guard kit you need for the disguise rather than raiding every chest a frightened refugee wouldn't touch.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Henry watches his parents die and barely makes it to Talmberg.</details>
- [ ] **3. [Homecoming](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Homecoming)** — `MAIN` — Return to Skalitz to bury your parents.
  - *Where:* Starts automatically at Talmberg after Run!; the road leads through Rovna to Skalitz.
  1. Leave Talmberg and follow the road through ruined Rovna to Skalitz.
  2. Get past the looters on the road without killing them — knock them down or outrun them.
  3. Reach Skalitz to bury your parents.
  - 🎭 Henry can barely hold a sword yet — knock the looters down rather than kill them.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven; the graveside fight plays out the same regardless. Sparing the road looters costs nothing – and the second one counts as a civilian murder if killed.
  - **Tip:** The looters crumble after a hit or two and surrender; sheathe your sword when they yield. Draw on Zbyshek and he bolts, leaving the spade.
  - **Immersion:** Walk, don't sprint, through Rovna and Skalitz and pause at the bodies. Bianca's ring is only available now – keeping it as a keepsake suits Henry; looting strangers doesn't.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Runt's men catch Henry at Skalitz; Theresa and the Talmberg men get him out alive.</details>
- [ ] **4. [Ordinary Routine](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Ordinary_Routine)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — A Woman's Lot begins: a normal morning as Theresa.
  - *Where:* Theresa at the Rattay Mill — ask "How did you manage to survive?" (A Woman's Lot DLC).
  1. As Theresa: weed the garden, feed the chickens and Tinker the dog.
  2. Talk to your father, then fetch nails from the blacksmith's wife.
  3. Find out what Bianca and Johanka want (this opens Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker).
  4. Report back to your father.
  - 🎭 No Cuman or bandit kills for all of A Woman's Lot — Theresa is fleeing, not fighting.
  - **Outcome:** Finishing every morning chore earns Father's full coin and pocket money; botching them costs reputation and cash. Confirming the dance at the end fails anything unfinished.
  - **Tip:** Do the chickens, Tinker and the weeding before speaking to Father. Wrap up Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker before agreeing to go to the dance.
  - **Immersion:** Play Theresa as a busy miller's daughter: chat with neighbours, don't loot the town. Tell Henry the beer is from you – a small, in-character nudge that also earns reputation.
- [ ] **5. [Cry Wolf!](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Cry_Wolf!)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Pick belladonna for Bianca.
  - *Where:* Bianca in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine (playing as Theresa).
  1. Bianca needs belladonna from the woods for her Saviour Schnapps.
  2. Pick the herbs; people say a wolf has been seen, so stay alert.
  3. Bring them back to Bianca.
  - **Outcome:** Give the magpie's ring to Bianca to keep her goodwill; keeping it boosts Theresa's charisma but sours Bianca. The wolf must be shot either way.
  - **Tip:** Borrow Adam's bow (with 20 arrows) by promising him a dance. When Bianca starts picking, face west – the wolf comes from that side.
  - **Immersion:** Killing a wolf is fine under the no-kills rule; it's an animal threatening a friend. Keep close to Bianca rather than wandering off to explore.
- [ ] **6. [Matchmaker](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Matchmaker)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Help Johanka get her dance invitation.
  - *Where:* Bianca / Johanka in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine.
  1. Help Johanka get Matthias to invite her to the village dance.
  2. Optional: bring Matthias's lucky dice to Johanka.
  3. Optional: get Fritz to teach you lockpicking.
  - **Outcome:** Telling Matthias that Johanka likes him pleases everyone. Just stealing the dice completes it but leaves Matthias sore; you can still pocket the lucky die for farkle.
  - **Tip:** Ask Fritz about lockpicking – it's the easy way to get Theresa some lockpicks before the day's events.
  - **Immersion:** A friend's favour, not a heist: talk first, and only lift the die if Johanka's plan needs it.
- [ ] **7. [The Family Silver](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Family_Silver)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Your father's errand at the Skalitz mill.
  - *Where:* Theresa's father at the Skalitz mill.
  1. Talk to your father at the Skalitz mill about the family silver.
  2. Follow his instructions and the quest markers.
  3. Report back before the day ends.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven DLC errand with no meaningful branches; completing it is required to finish Ordinary Routine.
  - **Tip:** Finish the optional Skalitz errands first; once this is done, the evening dance is the next step and agreeing to it fails anything left over.
  - **Immersion:** Theresa is covering for her family, not getting rich: take only what the task needs and keep it quiet around Janek and Jaroslav.
- [ ] **8. [Unexpected Visit (Theresa)](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Unexpected_Visit_%28Theresa%29)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — The raid, through Theresa's eyes.
  - *Where:* Theresa's father at the Skalitz mill, after the morning chores.
  1. Talk to Father; pick up the nails from the blacksmith.
  2. Optional: feed the chickens and the dog.
  3. The army arrives while you're out.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven; the raid and its outcome are fixed. Doing the optional chores only adds flavour.
  - **Tip:** The trunk by the stove has a bandage for battered Zbyshek. Stop at the tavern – Bianca hands over a Saviour Schnapps before things go wrong.
  - **Immersion:** Do the morning chores properly; it's Theresa's last ordinary morning and the contrast with what follows is the point.
- [ ] **9. [Thicker than Water](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Thicker_than_Water)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Escape to the mines and find Samuel.
  - *Where:* Follows Unexpected Visit (Theresa) automatically.
  1. Flee and shake off the attackers.
  2. Get to the mines and find Samuel.
  3. Optional: explore the side mineshafts.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven. There's no knockout option for the Cuman in the mill; sneaking past him through the window is the only way to keep the no-kills rule.
  - **Tip:** Crouch, leave the looting Cuman alone and slip out through the window into the barn. A Saviour Schnapps sits in the wheelbarrow outside the collapsed southern shaft.
  - **Immersion:** Theresa is fleeing, not fighting: hug the palisade, use the dark and keep Tinker close. No looting bodies for gear she can't use anyway.
- [ ] **10. [Ties that Bind](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Ties_that_Bind)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Patch up your wounded brother.
  - *Where:* Follows Thicker than Water, in the Skalitz mines.
  1. Treat Samuel's wounds.
  2. Gather 3 pieces of linen (or 6 bandages), 10 marigolds and water.
  3. Optional: send Tinker to "Seek!" to find things faster.
  - **Outcome:** The ending is fixed – nothing you do changes it, so don't reload to try. Scaring off the looter at the crashed wagon keeps the no-kills rule.
  - **Tip:** Water is right at the first junction inside the mine. The crashed wagon east has five marigolds and linen; pick the rest north on the east side of the road.
  - **Immersion:** Do it at night as the game sets it; move between cover. Franta's plea for mercy is optional – refusing fits a Theresa who isn't a killer.
- [ ] **11. [Aftermath](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Aftermath)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Search the ruins for survivors with Tinker.
  - *Where:* Follows Ties that Bind, outside the Skalitz mines.
  1. Search beyond the walls for survivors.
  2. Follow Tinker — he leads you to Holba (Angel of Mercy).
  3. Help the injured man, then keep looking for survivors.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven ending to Theresa's flashback; its key events can't be avoided. Rescuing every survivor along the way (Angel of Mercy) is the only real variable.
  - **Tip:** Rescue everyone in Angel of Mercy before going through Skalitz's north-east gate – that's where the finale begins.
  - **Immersion:** Pause at the mill on the way; Theresa sees her family there. Walk the ruins rather than sprinting through.
  - 🪙 Skalitz shield
- [ ] **12. [Angel of Mercy](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Angel_of_Mercy_%28quest%29)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` — Get every survivor you can to safety.
  - *Where:* Holba, found by following Tinker the dog at the start of Aftermath.
  1. Potter's smallholding: find Stanislav's stash and bring it to him.
  2. Near the works: help Jarmila escape.
  3. Woodcutters' camp: get Julian to flee.
  4. By the woods: get Marosh to safety; optionally find Pavlena for him.
  - **Recommended:** The deserter near Jarmila is optional. Scare him off or sneak past rather than kill, to keep the no-kills rule.
  - **Outcome:** Saving all five survivors (Holba, Stanislav, Jarmila, Julian, Marosh) earns the Saviour achievement. Lying to Stanislav's looter avoids a fight; handing over the coin works too.
  - **Tip:** Holba needs two bandages. Julian's axe is on a stump under the lean-to; Stanislav's keys lie by the spilled wood basket, his purse in the pantry's apple basket.
  - **Immersion:** Tell Marosh the truth about Pavlena rather than leaving him searching; that's Theresa's whole character here.
- [ ] **13. [The Good Thief](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Good_Thief)** — `SIDE` — Steal a hanged man's ring to pay Peshek back.
  - *Where:* Miller Peshek at the Rattay Mill — he wants his treatment paid for.
  - **Requires:** Spade · Lockpicks
  1. Peshek asks you to settle your treatment debt.
  2. Turn down his dodgy job for now.
  3. Accept Peshek's job.
  4. Learn lockpicking from Peshek.
  5. Get a spade; the hanged man's ring can be dug up, or found hidden in the executioner's house.
  6. Take the ring to the miller in Kohelnitz (Woyzeck).
  7. Optional: pay Peshek back for your treatment.
  - ℹ This opens the millers' Thick as Thieves jobs.
  - 🕯 Henry arrives with nothing: no armour, no sword of his own, no gold. Keep it that way until he earns it.
  - **Outcome:** Paying 100 groschen settles it honestly; the ring job opens the millers' fencing network and thief work. Once you've taken the job, paying as well gains nothing.
  - **Tip:** Digging the grave finds nothing – the ring is in an easy chest in Hermann's bedroom. Tell him Peshek owes him flour and he'll hurry off.
  - **Immersion:** Henry should resent this: do it once he's broke, at night, in dark clothes. Bring raw meat for Hermann's dogs rather than hurting them.
- [ ] **14. [Pickpocketing](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Pickpocketing_%28activity%29)** — `ACTIVITY` — Peshek teaches you to lift a purse.
  - *Where:* Miller Peshek at the Rattay Mill — ask him to teach you (after settling your debt).
  1. Go behind the mill with Peshek.
  2. Try to lift his dagger without getting caught.
  - **Outcome:** Simple training; Peshek always takes his dagger back. Repeatable whenever you want practice.
  - **Tip:** Crouch directly behind him and release before the circle turns red. If he wanders off, just talk to him to restart.
  - **Immersion:** Henry learns this reluctantly; it's a skill for later, not a habit. Avoid practising on random townsfolk afterwards.
- [ ] **15. [Thick as Thieves - Peshek](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Thick_as_Thieves_-_Peshek)** — `ACTIVITY` — Three thefts around Rattay for Peshek.
  - *Where:* Miller Peshek at the Rattay Mill, after The Good Thief.
  - **Requires:** Lockpicks · Lockpicking for Hard locks
  - **Bring:** Padfoot potion
  1. First job: steal the ring from the bathhouse madam's chest.
  2. Bring it to Peshek.
  3. Steal the documents from the Rattay Rathaus.
  4. Steal Sir Hanush's illuminated book from the upper castle.
  5. Bring everything to Peshek.
  - ⚠ Hard locks — bring lockpicks and a Padfoot potion.
  - **Outcome:** Pays roughly 150, 175 and 250 groschen. Hanush's book is the riskiest theft in the game early on; getting caught in the upper castle hurts reputation badly.
  - **Tip:** Rob the bathhouse chest by day while upstairs is empty. The deed is on a table in the bailiff's private chamber behind a Hard door; drink Padfoot first.
  - **Immersion:** Stealing from Hanush, Henry's own patron, is a big line – do it at night in dark clothes, and only once Henry has reasons to resent the town.
  - 🪙 150 groschen
- [ ] **16. [Thick as Thieves - Woyzeck](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Thick_as_Thieves_-_Woyzeck)** — `ACTIVITY` — Start the Kohelnitz miller's three jobs while you deliver the ring.
  - *Where:* Miller Woyzeck at the Kohelnitz mill, after The Good Thief.
  - **Requires:** Lockpicks · Lockpicking 15 — Very Hard chest
  - **Bring:** Padfoot potion at Lockpicking 10+
  1. Talk to Miller Woyzeck — you can take all three jobs at once.
  2. Steal at least five waffenrocks from the Talmberg armoury (getting the armoury key helps).
  3. Steal Lady Stephanie's jewellery.
  4. Steal a horse from the Merhojed stables.
  5. Ride it to Woyzeck.
  6. Hand over the waffenrocks and jewellery and ask Woyzeck for your reward.
  - ⚠ Bring lockpicks and Padfoot potions.
  - **Outcome:** Pays around 250 for the waffenrocks and 300 for the horse. Must be finished before the attack on Vranik in The Die is Cast, or it becomes impossible.
  - **Tip:** Stephanie's chest is Very Hard: Lockpicking 15, or 10 with Padfoot. Any Merhojed horse counts. The armoury key can be taken from a sleeping guard in the left tower.
  - **Immersion:** Stephanie once helped Henry – robbing her is a betrayal. Go at night in dark, quiet clothes and keep it out of Henry's later conversations with her.
  - 🪙 250 groschen
- [ ] **17. [Do Me a Favour - Punch Me!](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Do_Me_A_Favour_-_Punch_Me!)** — `ACTIVITY` — Beat the three refugee brawlers.
  - *Where:* Milan, among the Skalitz refugees in Rattay (Alex points you to the brawls).
  - **Bring:** Strength for winning clinches
  1. Find Milan's brawls among the refugees.
  2. Beat Ringlet, Stephen, then Milan.
  3. Fists only, no weapons.
  - **Outcome:** Beating all three unlocks repeat bouts – small wagers but excellent early strength, agility, unarmed and defence XP.
  - **Tip:** Wear a gambeson and helmet – the brawlers ignore armour, so you take little damage. Fight Milan aggressively to drain his stamina, then clinch.
  - **Immersion:** Fists only, fair play: don't loot your knocked-out opponents, even though the game lets you. Treat it as refugee lads letting off steam.
- [ ] **18. [Rob the Rich, Give to the Poor](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Rob_the_Rich,_Give_to_the_Poor)** — `ACTIVITY` — Pickpocket the wealthy for the Vagabond's refugees.
  - *Where:* The Vagabond (a Skalitz refugee) in Rattay; needs Pickpocketing first.
  - **Requires:** Pickpocketing (taught by Peshek)
  - **Bring:** Check noise of clothing
  1. The Vagabond asks you to steal for the refugees. Say no for now.
  2. Do all the Rattay parts: 50 groschen from the guard Pazdera, 50 from the rich woman, and Wolfram Pruda's silver ring.
  3. Bring each one back to the Vagabond.
  4. Steal the guild seal from the Ledetchko merchant.
  5. Bring the seal back to the Vagabond in Rattay.
  - **Outcome:** The Vagabond ends up in the stocks, so the final seal can't be handed in for pay – sell it to a miller. Earlier jobs split the coin.
  - **Tip:** Needs Pickpocketing first. Rob the rich woman while she sews with her back to the door.
  - **Immersion:** For the first two jobs you may pay the 50 groschen yourself instead of stealing – ideal if Henry still doubts the Vagabond's story.
  - 🪙 25 groschen
- [ ] **19. [Awakening](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Awakening)** — `MAIN` — Report to Sir Radzig at Pirkstein.
  - *Where:* Starts automatically — Henry wakes at the Rattay Mill.
  - **Bring:** Bribe or Speech at Rattay gate
  1. Settle up at the mill and go to Sir Radzig in the Upper Castle.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven, no choices. Not timed—you can explore before reporting to Radzig.
  - **Tip:** Fight the Cumans in the fever dream for a little XP. Bow training with Berthold stays locked until after Train Hard, Fight Easy.
  - **Immersion:** Henry's whole aim is Radzig and the sword—head up to Pirkstein once the debt is in hand, not after weeks of wandering.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Sir Radzig takes Henry into his service.</details>

> 🏁 **Checkpoint — end of Part 1:** Starting stats; no reading, horse or lockpicking yet. Avoid real fights.

## Part 2 — In Sir Radzig's Garrison

*Training, the hunt with Capon, and the Neuhof investigation up to finding Reeky.*

- [ ] **20. [Train Hard, Fight Easy](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Train_Hard,_Fight_Easy)** — `MAIN` — Combat training with Captain Bernard.
  - *Where:* Captain Bernard at the Rattay combat arena (north gate).
  1. Report to Captain Bernard at the combat arena.
  2. Learn moving, slashes, stabs, combos, then blocking.
  3. Finish the sparring test.
  - 🕯 Henry is finally in Radzig's service. Now is the time to equip proper armour and a real sword.
  - **Outcome:** Beat Hans at both archery and the duel to win his hunting bow; one win each means no exchange; lose both and he takes 2 groschen.
  - **Tip:** Archery: finish first for three bonus points and loose as soon as fully drawn. Duel: aim for Hans's bare head. Afterwards Bernard offers further training—take it.
  - **Immersion:** Show up in your own modest clothes; a blacksmith's son in stolen finery undercuts the scene with Hans.
  - 🪙 70 groschen
- [ ] **21. [Keeping the Peace](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Keeping_the_Peace)** — `MAIN` — Patrol Rattay with Nightingale.
  - *Where:* Sir Hanush after training; report to the watchman Nightingale.
  - **Bring:** Speech for persuasion
  1. Collect armour at the armoury, then report to Nightingale.
  2. Do the watch duties around Rattay.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven. Settle Beggar Jane's dispute with Speech or Charisma; intimidating the armoursmith always fails. The tavern brawl with Hans can't be won.
  - **Tip:** Wear at least a gambeson and helmet or Nightingale scolds you. While on the watch, guards don't search you for stolen goods.
  - **Immersion:** Carry a torch after dark as ordered, and give Jane alms yourself—the blacksmith's son knows charity.
- [ ] **22. [The Prey](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Prey)** — `MAIN` — Ride out hunting with Sir Hans Capon.
  - *Where:* Sir Hans Capon in the Upper Castle courtyard, from 5 a.m. (he gives up around 10–11).
  - **Bring:** Own horse to keep up · Light, quiet clothing
  1. Meet Sir Hans in the Upper Castle courtyard (5–10 a.m.) and ride out on the hunt.
  2. Protect him when the hunt goes wrong.
  - **Outcome:** Shoot one hare to win 100 groschen. Rescuing Hans earns Radzig's service and Pebbles.
  - **Tip:** Arrive mounted and Hans is merely dazed—straight fight. On foot you can return at night and take the sleeping Cumans quietly. Keep clear of Hans while hunting or lose 15.
  - **Immersion:** Wear quiet hunting clothes, and cook hares only after Hans has counted them.
  - 🪙 100 groschen
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Henry saves Hans Capon's life and earns his place in Radzig's service.</details>
- [ ] **23. [The Hunt Begins](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Hunt_Begins)** — `MAIN` — Investigate the Neuhof massacre with Bernard.
  - *Where:* Captain Bernard, straight after The Prey — he rides to Neuhof without waiting.
  - **Bring:** Leave with Bernard immediately · First Aid II perk
  - 🎒 **Prepare:** Be ready to ride with Bernard to Neuhof immediately; he won't wait. First Aid II lets you bandage Jakub.
  1. Ride with Captain Bernard to Neuhof straight away.
  2. Search the farm and the dead raiders.
  - **Outcome:** Riding out at once lets you overhear the bandits naming Ginger—extra dialogue later. Vanishing for over a day angers Bernard.
  - **Tip:** You must mount Pebbles even if you own a horse. Badassness always works on Mark. Search both bandits for the hoof-pick.
  - **Immersion:** Report each finding to Bernard before moving on—Henry is under his command now.
  - 🪙 100 groschen
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>A bloody hoof pick points to Ginger, the missing stableboy.</details>
- [ ] **24. [Ginger in a Pickle](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Ginger_in_a_Pickle)** — `MAIN` — Track down the missing stableboy.
  - *Where:* Starts at Neuhof after the hoof pick is found.
  - **Bring:** Speech or coin for spokesman
  1. Get Bernard's leave to carry on alone.
  2. Question the charcoal-burners and track Ginger north.
  3. Find Ginger and get the truth from him.
  - **Outcome:** Clear the bandits hunting Ginger and convince Zora: he returns home, later pays 150 plus Horsemanship XP, and you earn the 'Ginger' achievement.
  - **Tip:** Get Bernard's leave first. At the Talmberg charcoal-burners, follow the twin who slips away—he leads straight to Ginger. Bernard will help with the bandits if asked.
  - **Immersion:** Don't lie to Zora about 'no loose ends'—kill the bandits first so Henry's promise to Ginger is true.
  - 🪙 150 groschen
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Ginger names a limping raider from Uzhitz.</details>
- [ ] **25. [Mysterious Ways](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Mysterious_Ways)** — `MAIN` — Find Limpy Lubosh — and drink with Father Godwin.
  - *Where:* Starts after Ginger in a Pickle; go to Uzhitz.
  - **Bring:** Embrocation potion for scribe · Lockpicks — Very Hard door · Reading
  1. Talk your way in with the Uzhitz bailiff.
  2. Find Father Godwin and follow the trail.
  - **Recommended:** To get to Godwin's party (recommended), threaten him, then back down.
  - **Outcome:** Win Godwin's trust and preach well and he names the gang; fail and you need the Rattay scribe and an Embrocation instead.
  - **Tip:** Sermon: open confidently, then answer harshly twice; pick 'God stands at the head', 'To sin is human' and defend Godwin. Never draw steel in the tavern brawl.
  - **Immersion:** Search Lubosh's house properly first, and dress again after the binge before you preach.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>The trail leads to Reeky of Ledetchko.</details>
- [ ] **26. [On the Scent](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/On_the_Scent)** — `MAIN` ⏳ — Find Reeky before Runt's men do.
  - *Timed:* 3 days — Reeky dies if you're late
  - *Where:* Starts automatically after Mysterious Ways. Timed.
  - **Bring:** Go straight away — timed
  - 🎒 **Prepare:** On the Scent is timed: head out promptly or Runt's men reach Reeky first.
  1. Ask around Ledetchko about Reeky; talk to his father the tanner and to Adela at the bathhouse.
  2. Track Reeky down before Runt's men do.
  - ⚠ Timed — if you're slow, Runt's men get to Reeky first.
  - **Outcome:** Reach Reeky in time to hear the full story, then free him, kill him or take him to Rattay for trial. Arrive late and he dies.
  - **Tip:** Talk to the tanner first, then Adela—that order unlocks Raspberry's directions to the cave. Two bandits drink in the Ledetchko inn until 18:00; don't attack them in town.
  - **Immersion:** Handing Reeky over for trial is the lawful choice for one of Radzig's men.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Reeky points to Timmy near Merhojed.</details>

> 🏁 **Checkpoint — end of Part 2:** Bernard's training done: basic sword and shield work, a few potions, simple armour.

## Part 3 — Side Quests, Round One

*Mysterious Ways opens up most of the map's side content. Clear the Rattay, Talmberg and Uzhitz jobs before Pribyslavitz.*

- [ ] **27. [Chumps on the River](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Chumps_on_the_River)** — `ACTIVITY` — Vatzek's archery contest at Ledetchko.
  - *Where:* Vatzek at the Ledetchko archery range (tip from the Ledetchko innkeeper).
  - **Bring:** Light, fast bow · Horse to get closer
  1. Talk to Vatzek at the Ledetchko archery range.
