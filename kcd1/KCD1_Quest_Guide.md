# Kingdom Come: Deliverance — Chronological Role-Play Quest Guide

KCD1 (2018) with all four story DLCs, in the order of the Reddit role-play timeline. 114 visits, 134 quests, 268 checkpoints. Links go to kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg.

**Legend:** ⚠ warning/timed · 🔒 missable · 🎭 role-play note · 🕯 immersion · 💻 PC only · ℹ info · 🪙 reward

## 🕯 The Immersion Charter

*Self-imposed habits that keep Henry feeling like Henry. Pick the ones you like — none are required.*

**Before you start**

- Hardcore Mode (new game only): no compass, no player marker on the map, and negative perks you pick. It's the biggest immersion boost, and it's harder.
- Learn to read the map by landmarks — roads, rivers, church towers — instead of following the quest marker.

**Living as Henry**

- Sleep at night in a bed you own or have rented. Eat proper meals instead of waiting for the hunger warning.
- Wash and mend your clothes before meeting nobles. Charisma changes how people react, and a bloody Henry at Radzig's table makes no sense.
- Save sparingly: sleep in your own bed or drink Saviour Schnapps only before genuinely risky moments.
- Walk inside towns, carry a torch at night, and only draw a weapon when there's a reason to.

**Gear that fits the story**

- Before Awakening, Henry is a penniless refugee: plain clothes and a cheap weapon, no shining plate.
- Upgrade to real armour once Radzig takes you into service — bought, earned or taken from bandits, not stripped from honest townsfolk.
- Keep Cuman gear for Masquerade and Nest of Vipers. Wearing it around villages otherwise makes no sense.
- Dress for the occasion: noble clothes for Capon's errands, a habit in the monastery, hunting gear in the woods.

**Travel**

- Fast travel is fair game, since time passes and ambushes happen. Still, ride the road for the big moments: the flight to Talmberg, Pribyslavitz, Vranik.
- Don't climb cliffs on horseback or jump walls to skip a gate.

**Crime & conscience**

- Steal only when Henry has a reason: the Peshek arc, charity for the refugees, or a quest. Never rob friends who helped him.
- Let surrendering enemies flee. Don't strip unconscious townsfolk.
- Henry is a blacksmith's son, not a killer. Keep early fights to fists and knock-outs where you can.

**Pacing**

- Don't rush the main story after Awakening. The route deliberately spreads side jobs between main beats.
- Finish timed quests in one go. They're written as urgent, so play them that way.
- Respect NPC routines: shops in the day, nobles in the morning, rest at night.
- Don't skip cutscenes or dialogue — that's where Henry's growth happens.

*Collected from long-standing r/kingdomcome role-play advice and the KCD1 wiki. Reddit blocked live access while this guide was built, so treat these as community conventions, not hard rules.*

## Act I — Prologue & A Woman's Lot

*Skalitz before and during the attack. Henry can barely hold a sword: no Cuman or bandit kills.*

### Visit 1 — Intro

*Live Henry's last day in Skalitz, flee to Talmberg, then replay the sack through Theresa's eyes.*

> ⚠ **RP rule for the whole prologue and A Woman's Lot: no Cuman or bandit kills. Knock out, flee, or sneak instead.**
> ℹ **A Woman's Lot unlocks in-game only after Awakening (ask Theresa at the Rattay Mill "How did you manage to survive?"). The route places it here because that's when it happens in the story — play it as soon as it unlocks.**

- [ ] **[Unexpected Visit](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Unexpected_Visit)** — `MAIN` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Starts automatically — Henry wakes at home in Skalitz; his father Martin is at the forge.
  1. Eat breakfast, then see Martin at the forge — he needs supplies to finish Sir Radzig's sword.
  2. Help friends: join Fritz, Matthew and the lads in their prank on the German's house.
  3. Get help from friends: bring them along when you squeeze Kunesh for the money he owes.
  4. Buy the charcoal and beer, then help Martin finish the sword.
  - **Choice:** Do both friend objectives — they set up the Skalitz friendships the route uses later.
  - **Outcome:** Tutorial with no lasting branches. Joining the dung prank earns a scolding from Martin but nothing worse; skipping it costs only some early friendship flavour.
  - **Tip:** Mother's opening questions set starting stats. Answer 'They are not drunks', then 'Give me an example' to unlock Martin's Kuttenberg talk. Buy the ale last – it warms.
  - **Immersion:** Let Henry be a lazy, cocky village lad here: sleep-heavy, eager for fights and pranks. Don't strip the house or steal the charcoal; that's not yet who he is.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Sigismund's army, Cumans included, falls on Skalitz without warning.</details>
- [ ] **[Run!](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Run!)** — `MAIN` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Starts automatically when the army reaches Skalitz.
  1. Grab what you can at home and take Radzig's sword.
  2. Escape the town: the gates are shut, so make for the stables.
  3. Ride for Talmberg with Cumans on your heels and warn the castle.
  - 🎭 Don't fight — Henry is a blacksmith's son, not a soldier.
  - **Outcome:** Whistling to scare the Cumans off Theresa changes her later dialogue for the better. Asking Lady Stephanie for help is the cleanest escape; pleading with Robard costs reputation.
  - **Tip:** Ride without stopping – the arrow wound kills you if you dawdle. Leaving Talmberg: Stephanie points you to a guard disguise, then talk Radim into opening the gate.
  - **Immersion:** Never draw on the Cumans. At Talmberg, take only the guard kit you need for the disguise rather than raiding every chest a frightened refugee wouldn't touch.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Henry watches his parents die and barely makes it to Talmberg.</details>
- [ ] **[Homecoming](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Homecoming)** — `MAIN` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Starts automatically at Talmberg after Run!; the road leads through Rovna to Skalitz.
  1. Leave Talmberg and follow the road through ruined Rovna to Skalitz.
  2. Get past the looters on the road without killing them — knock them down or outrun them.
  3. Reach Skalitz to bury your parents.
  - 🎭 No kills. Henry couldn't hold his own against a stick yesterday; he isn't killing bandits today.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven; the graveside fight plays out the same regardless. Sparing the road looters costs nothing – and the second one counts as a civilian murder if killed.
  - **Tip:** The looters crumble after a hit or two and surrender; sheathe your sword when they yield. Draw on Zbyshek and he bolts, leaving the spade.
  - **Immersion:** Walk, don't sprint, through Rovna and Skalitz and pause at the bodies. Bianca's ring is only available now – keeping it as a keepsake suits Henry; looting strangers doesn't.
  - <details><summary>Story spoiler</summary>Runt's men catch Henry at Skalitz; Theresa and the Talmberg men get him out alive.</details>
- [ ] **[Ordinary Routine](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Ordinary_Routine)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Theresa at the Rattay Mill — ask "How did you manage to survive?" (needs the A Woman's Lot DLC; unlocks after Awakening).
  1. As Theresa: weed the garden, feed the chickens and Tinker the dog.
  2. Talk to your father, then fetch nails from the blacksmith's wife.
  3. Find out what Bianca and Johanka want (this opens Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker).
  4. Report back to your father.
  - ⚠ No Cuman or bandit kills for all of A Woman's Lot.
  - **Outcome:** Finishing every morning chore earns Father's full coin and pocket money; botching them costs reputation and cash. Confirming the dance at the end fails anything unfinished.
  - **Tip:** Do the chickens, Tinker and the weeding before speaking to Father. Wrap up Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker before agreeing to go to the dance.
  - **Immersion:** Play Theresa as a busy miller's daughter: chat with neighbours, don't loot the town. Tell Henry the beer is from you – a small, in-character nudge that also earns reputation.
- [ ] **[Cry Wolf!](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Cry_Wolf!)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Bianca in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine (playing as Theresa).
  1. Bianca needs belladonna from the woods for her Saviour Schnapps.
  2. Pick the herbs; people say a wolf has been seen, so stay alert.
  3. Bring them back to Bianca.
  - **Outcome:** Give the magpie's ring to Bianca to keep her goodwill; keeping it boosts Theresa's charisma but sours Bianca. The wolf must be shot either way.
  - **Tip:** Borrow Adam's bow (with 20 arrows) by promising him a dance. When Bianca starts picking, face west – the wolf comes from that side.
  - **Immersion:** Killing a wolf is fine under the no-kills rule; it's an animal threatening a friend. Keep close to Bianca rather than wandering off to explore.
- [ ] **[Matchmaker](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Matchmaker)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Bianca / Johanka in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine.
  1. Help Johanka get Matthias to invite her to the village dance.
  2. Optional: bring Matthias's lucky dice to Johanka.
  3. Optional: get Fritz to teach you lockpicking.
  - **Outcome:** Telling Matthias that Johanka likes him pleases everyone. Just stealing the dice completes it but leaves Matthias sore; you can still pocket the lucky die for farkle.
  - **Tip:** Ask Fritz about lockpicking – it's the easy way to get Theresa some lockpicks before the day's events.
  - **Immersion:** A friend's favour, not a heist: talk first, and only lift the die if Johanka's plan needs it.
- [ ] **[The Family Silver](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Family_Silver)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Theresa's father at the Skalitz mill.
  1. Talk to your father at the Skalitz mill about the family silver.
  2. Follow his instructions and the quest markers.
  3. Report back before the day ends.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven DLC errand with no meaningful branches; completing it is required to finish Ordinary Routine.
  - **Tip:** Finish the optional Skalitz errands first; once this is done, the evening dance is the next step and agreeing to it fails anything left over.
  - **Immersion:** Theresa is covering for her family, not getting rich: take only what the task needs and keep it quiet around Janek and Jaroslav.
- [ ] **[Unexpected Visit (Theresa)](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Unexpected_Visit_%28Theresa%29)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Theresa's father at the Skalitz mill, after the morning chores.
  1. Talk to Father; pick up the nails from the blacksmith.
  2. Optional: feed the chickens and the dog.
  3. The army arrives while you're out.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven; the raid and its outcome are fixed. Doing the optional chores only adds flavour.
  - **Tip:** The trunk by the stove has a bandage for battered Zbyshek. Stop at the tavern – Bianca hands over a Saviour Schnapps before things go wrong.
  - **Immersion:** Do the morning chores properly; it's Theresa's last ordinary morning and the contrast with what follows is the point.
- [ ] **[Thicker than Water](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Thicker_than_Water)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Follows Unexpected Visit (Theresa) automatically.
  1. Flee and shake off the attackers.
  2. Get to the mines and find Samuel.
  3. Optional: explore the side mineshafts.
  - 🎭 Hide and run — no kills.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven. There's no knockout option for the Cuman in the mill; sneaking past him through the window is the only way to keep the no-kills rule.
  - **Tip:** Crouch, leave the looting Cuman alone and slip out through the window into the barn. A Saviour Schnapps sits in the wheelbarrow outside the collapsed southern shaft.
  - **Immersion:** Theresa is fleeing, not fighting: hug the palisade, use the dark and keep Tinker close. No looting bodies for gear she can't use anyway.
- [ ] **[Ties that Bind](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Ties_that_Bind)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Follows Thicker than Water, in the Skalitz mines.
  1. Treat Samuel's wounds.
  2. Gather 3 pieces of linen (or 6 bandages), 10 marigolds and water.
  3. Optional: send Tinker to "Seek!" to find things faster.
  - **Outcome:** The ending is fixed – nothing you do changes it, so don't reload to try. Scaring off the looter at the crashed wagon keeps the no-kills rule.
  - **Tip:** Water is right at the first junction inside the mine. The crashed wagon east has five marigolds and linen; pick the rest north on the east side of the road.
  - **Immersion:** Do it at night as the game sets it; move between cover. Franta's plea for mercy is optional – refusing fits a Theresa who isn't a killer.
- [ ] **[Aftermath](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Aftermath)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Follows Ties that Bind, outside the Skalitz mines.
  1. Search beyond the walls for survivors.
  2. Follow Tinker — he leads you to Holba (Angel of Mercy).
  3. Help the injured man, then keep looking for survivors.
  - **Outcome:** Story-driven ending to Theresa's flashback; its key events can't be avoided. Rescuing every survivor along the way (Angel of Mercy) is the only real variable.
  - **Tip:** Rescue everyone in Angel of Mercy before going through Skalitz's north-east gate – that's where the finale begins.
  - **Immersion:** Pause at the mill when the route passes it; Theresa sees her family there. Walk the ruins rather than sprinting through.
  - 🪙 Skalitz shield
- [ ] **[Angel of Mercy](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Angel_of_Mercy_%28quest%29)** — `DLC: A Woman's Lot` · **Complete**
  - *Where:* Holba, found by following Tinker the dog at the start of Aftermath.
  1. Potter's smallholding: find Stanislav's stash and bring it to him.
  2. Near the works: help Jarmila escape.
  3. Woodcutters' camp: get Julian to flee.
  4. By the woods: get Marosh to safety; optionally find Pavlena for him.
  - **Choice:** The deserter near Jarmila is optional. Scare him off or sneak past rather than kill, to keep the no-kills rule.
  - **Outcome:** Saving all five survivors (Holba, Stanislav, Jarmila, Julian, Marosh) earns the Saviour achievement. Lying to Stanislav's looter avoids a fight; handing over the coin works too.
  - **Tip:** Holba needs two bandages. Julian's axe is on a stump under the lean-to; Stanislav's keys lie by the spilled wood basket, his purse in the pantry's apple basket.
  - **Immersion:** Tell Marosh the truth about Pavlena rather than leaving him searching; that's Theresa's whole character here.

> 🏁 **Checkpoint — end of Act I:** Starting stats: no reading, no lockpicking, no horse. Avoid fights — Theresa's chapter is pitched around level 4–5.

### Visit 2 — Rattay #1

*First look around Rattay: refuse the shady jobs for now, meet the Skalitz refugees.*

> 🕯 *Henry arrives with nothing: no armour, no sword of his own, no gold. Keep it that way until he earns it.*

- [ ] **[The Good Thief](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Good_Thief)** — `SIDE` · **Refuse for now**
  - *Where:* Miller Peshek at the Rattay Mill — he wants his treatment paid for.
  - **Requires:** Spade · Lockpicks
  1. Peshek asks you to settle your treatment debt.
  2. Turn down his dodgy job for now.
  - 🎭 Henry is honest at heart — he only gives in later, once he's out of options.
  - **Outcome:** Paying 100 groschen settles it honestly; the ring job opens the millers' fencing network and thief work. Once you've taken the job, paying as well gains nothing.
  - **Tip:** Digging the grave finds nothing – the ring is in an easy chest in Hermann's bedroom. Tell him Peshek owes him flour and he'll hurry off.
  - **Immersion:** Henry should resent this: do it once he's broke, at night, in dark clothes. Bring raw meat for Hermann's dogs rather than hurting them.

### 5. Link verification & open questions

All 138 wiki links were checked against kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg and resolve. The six Madonna of Sasau chapters without their own page link to verified section anchors on the Madonna page. Unverified links: none.

Things to be aware of:

- Emergency Supply: the wiki says it only appears if you sided with the poachers in Sheep in Wolf's Clothing. This route sides with the guards, so it may never trigger.
- Vengeance: the banner text follows the brief ("finishes at the Epilogue"), but the wiki says it stays open at the end of the game.
- A Woman's Lot unlocks only after Awakening in-game; the route places it in the prologue for story order.
- Band of Bastards is never marked Complete in the route; the guide closes it at Kuno's Camp #1, when Bad Blood begins.
- Poverty, Chastity and Obedience has no Complete line in the route; the guide closes it at Rattay #21, just before the monastery.
- The Family Silver has almost no wiki detail, so its steps are general.
- Open-ended activities (Chumps on the River, The Queen of Sheba's Sword, Fat Profits, Green-Eyed Monster, Skin) have no completion point in the route.
