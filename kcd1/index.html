The Immersion Charter

Self-imposed habits that keep Henry feeling like Henry. Pick the ones you like — none are required.

Before you start

  • Hardcore Mode (new game only): no compass, no player marker on the map, and negative perks you pick. It's the biggest immersion boost, and it's harder.
  • Learn to read the map by landmarks — roads, rivers, church towers — instead of following the quest marker.

Living as Henry

  • Sleep at night in a bed you own or have rented. Eat proper meals instead of waiting for the hunger warning.
  • Wash and mend your clothes before meeting nobles. Charisma changes how people react, and a bloody Henry at Radzig's table makes no sense.
  • Save sparingly: sleep in your own bed or drink Saviour Schnapps only before genuinely risky moments.
  • Walk inside towns, carry a torch at night, and only draw a weapon when there's a reason to.

Gear that fits the story

  • Before Awakening, Henry is a penniless refugee: plain clothes and a cheap weapon, no shining plate.
  • Upgrade to real armour once Radzig takes you into service — bought, earned or taken from bandits, not stripped from honest townsfolk.
  • Keep Cuman gear for Masquerade and Nest of Vipers. Wearing it around villages otherwise makes no sense.
  • Dress for the occasion: noble clothes for Capon's errands, a habit in the monastery, hunting gear in the woods.

Travel

  • Fast travel is fair game, since time passes and ambushes happen. Still, ride the road for the big moments: the flight to Talmberg, Pribyslavitz, Vranik.
  • Don't climb cliffs on horseback or jump walls to skip a gate.

Crime & conscience

  • Steal only when Henry has a reason: the Peshek arc, charity for the refugees, or a quest. Never rob friends who helped him.
  • Let surrendering enemies flee. Don't strip unconscious townsfolk.
  • Henry is a blacksmith's son, not a killer. Keep early fights to fists and knock-outs where you can.

Pacing

  • Don't rush the main story after Awakening. The route deliberately spreads side jobs between main beats.
  • Finish timed quests in one go. They're written as urgent, so play them that way.
  • Respect NPC routines: shops in the day, nobles in the morning, rest at night.
  • Don't skip cutscenes or dialogue — that's where Henry's growth happens.

Collected from long-standing r/kingdomcome role-play advice and the KCD1 wiki. Reddit blocked live access while this guide was built, so treat these as community conventions, not hard rules.

Unexpected Visit

MAINComplete

Where to findStarts automatically — Henry wakes at home in Skalitz; his father Martin is at the forge.

  1. Eat breakfast, then see Martin at the forge — he needs supplies to finish Sir Radzig's sword.
  2. Help friends: join Fritz, Matthew and the lads in their prank on the German's house.
  3. Get help from friends: bring them along when you squeeze Kunesh for the money he owes.
  4. Buy the charcoal and beer, then help Martin finish the sword.
ChoiceDo both friend objectives — they set up the Skalitz friendships the route uses later.
Outcome

Tutorial with no lasting branches. Joining the dung prank earns a scolding from Martin but nothing worse; skipping it costs only some early friendship flavour.

Tip

Mother's opening questions set starting stats. Answer 'They are not drunks', then 'Give me an example' to unlock Martin's Kuttenberg talk. Buy the ale last – it warms.

Immersion

Let Henry be a lazy, cocky village lad here: sleep-heavy, eager for fights and pranks. Don't strip the house or steal the charcoal; that's not yet who he is.

Reveal story outcome

Sigismund's army, Cumans included, falls on Skalitz without warning.

Run!

MAINComplete

Where to findStarts automatically when the army reaches Skalitz.

  1. Grab what you can at home and take Radzig's sword.
  2. Escape the town: the gates are shut, so make for the stables.
  3. Ride for Talmberg with Cumans on your heels and warn the castle.
Role-playDon't fight — Henry is a blacksmith's son, not a soldier.
Outcome

Whistling to scare the Cumans off Theresa changes her later dialogue for the better. Asking Lady Stephanie for help is the cleanest escape; pleading with Robard costs reputation.

Tip

Ride without stopping – the arrow wound kills you if you dawdle. Leaving Talmberg: Stephanie points you to a guard disguise, then talk Radim into opening the gate.

Immersion

Never draw on the Cumans. At Talmberg, take only the guard kit you need for the disguise rather than raiding every chest a frightened refugee wouldn't touch.

Reveal story outcome

Henry watches his parents die and barely makes it to Talmberg.

Homecoming

MAINComplete

Where to findStarts automatically at Talmberg after Run!; the road leads through Rovna to Skalitz.

  1. Leave Talmberg and follow the road through ruined Rovna to Skalitz.
  2. Get past the looters on the road without killing them — knock them down or outrun them.
  3. Reach Skalitz to bury your parents.
Role-playNo kills. Henry couldn't hold his own against a stick yesterday; he isn't killing bandits today.
Outcome

Story-driven; the graveside fight plays out the same regardless. Sparing the road looters costs nothing – and the second one counts as a civilian murder if killed.

Tip

The looters crumble after a hit or two and surrender; sheathe your sword when they yield. Draw on Zbyshek and he bolts, leaving the spade.

Immersion

Walk, don't sprint, through Rovna and Skalitz and pause at the bodies. Bianca's ring is only available now – keeping it as a keepsake suits Henry; looting strangers doesn't.

Reveal story outcome

Runt's men catch Henry at Skalitz; Theresa and the Talmberg men get him out alive.

Ordinary Routine

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findTheresa at the Rattay Mill — ask "How did you manage to survive?" (needs the A Woman's Lot DLC; unlocks after Awakening).

  1. As Theresa: weed the garden, feed the chickens and Tinker the dog.
  2. Talk to your father, then fetch nails from the blacksmith's wife.
  3. Find out what Bianca and Johanka want (this opens Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker).
  4. Report back to your father.
WarningNo Cuman or bandit kills for all of A Woman's Lot.
Outcome

Finishing every morning chore earns Father's full coin and pocket money; botching them costs reputation and cash. Confirming the dance at the end fails anything unfinished.

Tip

Do the chickens, Tinker and the weeding before speaking to Father. Wrap up Cry Wolf! and Matchmaker before agreeing to go to the dance.

Immersion

Play Theresa as a busy miller's daughter: chat with neighbours, don't loot the town. Tell Henry the beer is from you – a small, in-character nudge that also earns reputation.

Cry Wolf!

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findBianca in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine (playing as Theresa).

  1. Bianca needs belladonna from the woods for her Saviour Schnapps.
  2. Pick the herbs; people say a wolf has been seen, so stay alert.
  3. Bring them back to Bianca.
Outcome

Give the magpie's ring to Bianca to keep her goodwill; keeping it boosts Theresa's charisma but sours Bianca. The wolf must be shot either way.

Tip

Borrow Adam's bow (with 20 arrows) by promising him a dance. When Bianca starts picking, face west – the wolf comes from that side.

Immersion

Killing a wolf is fine under the no-kills rule; it's an animal threatening a friend. Keep close to Bianca rather than wandering off to explore.

Matchmaker

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findBianca / Johanka in Skalitz, during Ordinary Routine.

  1. Help Johanka get Matthias to invite her to the village dance.
  2. Optional: bring Matthias's lucky dice to Johanka.
  3. Optional: get Fritz to teach you lockpicking.
Outcome

Telling Matthias that Johanka likes him pleases everyone. Just stealing the dice completes it but leaves Matthias sore; you can still pocket the lucky die for farkle.

Tip

Ask Fritz about lockpicking – it's the easy way to get Theresa some lockpicks before the day's events.

Immersion

A friend's favour, not a heist: talk first, and only lift the die if Johanka's plan needs it.

  2. Follow his instructions and the quest markers.

Story-driven DLC errand with no meaningful branches; completing it is required to finish Ordinary Routine.

Unexpected Visit (Theresa)

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findTheresa's father at the Skalitz mill, after the morning chores.

  1. Talk to Father; pick up the nails from the blacksmith.
  2. Optional: feed the chickens and the dog.
  3. The army arrives while you're out.
Outcome

Story-driven; the raid and its outcome are fixed. Doing the optional chores only adds flavour.

Tip

The trunk by the stove has a bandage for battered Zbyshek. Stop at the tavern – Bianca hands over a Saviour Schnapps before things go wrong.

Immersion

Do the morning chores properly; it's Theresa's last ordinary morning and the contrast with what follows is the point.

Thicker than Water

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findFollows Unexpected Visit (Theresa) automatically.

  1. Flee and shake off the attackers.
  2. Get to the mines and find Samuel.
  3. Optional: explore the side mineshafts.
Role-playHide and run — no kills.
Outcome

Story-driven. There's no knockout option for the Cuman in the mill; sneaking past him through the window is the only way to keep the no-kills rule.

Tip

Crouch, leave the looting Cuman alone and slip out through the window into the barn. A Saviour Schnapps sits in the wheelbarrow outside the collapsed southern shaft.

Immersion

Theresa is fleeing, not fighting: hug the palisade, use the dark and keep Tinker close. No looting bodies for gear she can't use anyway.

Ties that Bind

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findFollows Thicker than Water, in the Skalitz mines.

  1. Treat Samuel's wounds.
  2. Gather 3 pieces of linen (or 6 bandages), 10 marigolds and water.
  3. Optional: send Tinker to "Seek!" to find things faster.
Outcome

The ending is fixed – nothing you do changes it, so don't reload to try. Scaring off the looter at the crashed wagon keeps the no-kills rule.

Tip

Water is right at the first junction inside the mine. The crashed wagon east has five marigolds and linen; pick the rest north on the east side of the road.

Immersion

Do it at night as the game sets it; move between cover. Franta's plea for mercy is optional – refusing fits a Theresa who isn't a killer.

Aftermath

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findFollows Ties that Bind, outside the Skalitz mines.

  1. Search beyond the walls for survivors.
  2. Follow Tinker — he leads you to Holba (Angel of Mercy).
  3. Help the injured man, then keep looking for survivors.
Outcome

Story-driven ending to Theresa's flashback; its key events can't be avoided. Rescuing every survivor along the way (Angel of Mercy) is the only real variable.

Tip

Rescue everyone in Angel of Mercy before going through Skalitz's north-east gate – that's where the finale begins.

Immersion

Pause at the mill when the route passes it; Theresa sees her family there. Walk the ruins rather than sprinting through.

RewardSkalitz shield

Angel of Mercy

DLC: A Woman's LotComplete

Where to findHolba, found by following Tinker the dog at the start of Aftermath.

  1. Potter's smallholding: find Stanislav's stash and bring it to him.
  2. Near the works: help Jarmila escape.
  3. Woodcutters' camp: get Julian to flee.
  4. By the woods: get Marosh to safety; optionally find Pavlena for him.
ChoiceThe deserter near Jarmila is optional. Scare him off or sneak past rather than kill, to keep the no-kills rule.
Outcome

Saving all five survivors (Holba, Stanislav, Jarmila, Julian, Marosh) earns the Saviour achievement. Lying to Stanislav's looter avoids a fight; handing over the coin works too.

Tip

Holba needs two bandages. Julian's axe is on a stump under the lean-to; Stanislav's keys lie by the spilled wood basket, his purse in the pantry's apple basket.

Immersion

Tell Marosh the truth about Pavlena rather than leaving him searching; that's Theresa's whole character here.

Checkpoint · end of Act I

Starting stats: no reading, no lockpicking, no horse. Avoid fights — Theresa's chapter is pitched around level 4–5.

2. Activities & collectibles

  • Treasures of the Past — Five ancient treasure maps lead to five hidden chests holding the Warhorse armour set. The maps turn up as loot around the world; once you have one, follow its landmarks and dig. Pick them up whenever you come across them — nothing is missable.
  • Rattay Tourney — Enrol with the herald in Rattay on a tourney morning. Repeatable. First in the route: Visit 45.
  • Who Do You Like — Bet on yourself with Peshek before the tourney. Repeatable. Visit 45.
  • Capon's Champion — Fight the Rattay tourney on Sir Hans's behalf. Visit 95.
  • Talmberg Horse Race — One-off race set up through The Sport of Kings. Visit 60.
  • Dice — Play dice in any tavern. Game of Throws (Visit 95) has a dice tournament; Next to Godliness needs a game with Zdena.
  • Chumps on the River — Vatzek's archery contest at Ledetchko. Started at Visit 3; it's open-ended, so shoot whenever you pass through.
  • Fight Club & Revenge of the Cuman King — Do Me a Favour - Punch Me! (Visit 2) and Fishy Business in Uzhitz (Visit 52) are bare-knuckle brawls. Join the Sasau fight club in If You Can't Beat 'em (Visit 101), get Unarmed to 8, then beat the King of the Cumans in Skalitz (Visit 110).
  • The Ring of Bacchus — Band of Bastards drinking game, running from Visit 77 to Visit 83.
  • Open-ended jobs — The Queen of Sheba's Sword, Fat Profits, Green-Eyed Monster and Skin are started in the route but never marked done — finish them at your own pace.

5. Link verification & open questions

All 138 wiki links were checked against kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg and resolve. The six Madonna of Sasau chapters without their own page link to verified section anchors on the Madonna page. Unverified links: none.

Things to be aware of:

  • Emergency Supply: the wiki says it only appears if you sided with the poachers in Sheep in Wolf's Clothing. This route sides with the guards, so it may never trigger.
  • Vengeance: the banner text follows the brief ("finishes at the Epilogue"), but the wiki says it stays open at the end of the game.
  • A Woman's Lot unlocks only after Awakening in-game; the route places it in the prologue for story order.
  • Band of Bastards is never marked Complete in the route; the guide closes it at Kuno's Camp #1, when Bad Blood begins.
  • Poverty, Chastity and Obedience has no Complete line in the route; the guide closes it at Rattay #21, just before the monastery.
  • The Family Silver has almost no wiki detail, so its steps are general.
  • Open-ended activities (Chumps on the River, The Queen of Sheba's Sword, Fat Profits, Green-Eyed Monster, Skin) have no completion point in the route.
