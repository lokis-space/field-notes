2. Activities & collectibles

  • Treasures of the Past — Five ancient treasure maps lead to five hidden chests holding the Warhorse armour set. The maps turn up as loot around the world; once you have one, follow its landmarks and dig. Pick them up whenever you come across them — nothing is missable.
  • Rattay Tourney — Enrol with the herald in Rattay on a tourney morning. Repeatable. First in the route: Visit 45.
  • Who Do You Like — Bet on yourself with Peshek before the tourney. Repeatable. Visit 45.
  • Capon's Champion — Fight the Rattay tourney on Sir Hans's behalf. Visit 95.
  • Talmberg Horse Race — One-off race set up through The Sport of Kings. Visit 60.
  • Dice — Play dice in any tavern. Game of Throws (Visit 95) has a dice tournament; Next to Godliness needs a game with Zdena.
  • Chumps on the River — Vatzek's archery contest at Ledetchko. Started at Visit 3; it's open-ended, so shoot whenever you pass through.
  • Fight Club & Revenge of the Cuman King — Do Me a Favour - Punch Me! (Visit 2) and Fishy Business in Uzhitz (Visit 52) are bare-knuckle brawls. Join the Sasau fight club in If You Can't Beat 'em (Visit 101), get Unarmed to 8, then beat the King of the Cumans in Skalitz (Visit 110).
  • The Ring of Bacchus — Band of Bastards drinking game, running from Visit 77 to Visit 83.
  • Open-ended jobs — The Queen of Sheba's Sword, Fat Profits, Green-Eyed Monster and Skin are started in the route but never marked done — finish them at your own pace.

4. Missables & points of no return (in order)

  1. Visit 1 · A Woman's Lot — Prologue rule: no Cuman or bandit kills.
  2. Visit 20 · In God's Hands — Timed — don't wander away from the infirmary jobs for long.
  3. Visit 19 · Restless Spirit — Don't take the torn pages or other books near the Necronomicon.
  4. Visit 39 · Besmirched — Give everything back to Adam or lose him as a Pribyslavitz recruit.
  5. Visit 45 · Money for Old Rope — Timed — about one week.
  6. Visit 48 · On the Scent — Timed — find Reeky before Runt's men do.
  7. Visit 49 · My Friend Timmy — Don't kill Timmy yourself or you lose Mirka as a recruit.
  8. Visit 55 · Sheep in Wolf's Clothing — Intended fail (siding with the guards).
  9. Visit 54 · Hare Hunt — Intended fail (Cherchez la Femme overrides it).
  10. Visit 56 · Emergency Supply — Timed (2 days), and may never trigger on this route.
  11. Visit 57 · Gallows Brothers — Never complete it — it auto-fails at The Die is Cast.
  12. Visit 84 · All that Glisters — Stash your loot before questioning Rapota.
  13. Visit 95 · Next to Godliness — Timed — runs until morning.
  14. Visit 101 · If You Can't Beat 'em — Join the fight club via the Sasau tanner or Revenge of the Cuman King is locked out.
  15. Visit 104 · A Needle in a Haystack — Shave and reset your hair first — pre-rendered cutscenes from here on.
  16. Visit 104 · The Die is Cast — POINT OF NO RETURN — entering Vranik locks out several side quests.

5. Link verification & open questions

All 138 wiki links were checked against kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg and resolve. The six Madonna of Sasau chapters without their own page link to verified section anchors on the Madonna page. Unverified links: none.

Things to be aware of:

  • Emergency Supply: the wiki says it only appears if you sided with the poachers in Sheep in Wolf's Clothing. This route sides with the guards, so it may never trigger.
  • Vengeance: the banner text follows the brief ("finishes at the Epilogue"), but the wiki says it stays open at the end of the game.
  • A Woman's Lot unlocks only after Awakening in-game; the route places it in the prologue for story order.
  • Band of Bastards is never marked Complete in the route; the guide closes it at Kuno's Camp #1, when Bad Blood begins.
  • Poverty, Chastity and Obedience has no Complete line in the route; the guide closes it at Rattay #21, just before the monastery.
  • The Family Silver has almost no wiki detail, so its steps are general.
  • Open-ended activities (Chumps on the River, The Queen of Sheba's Sword, Fat Profits, Green-Eyed Monster, Skin) have no completion point in the route.
